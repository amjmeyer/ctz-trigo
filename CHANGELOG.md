# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-31)

First version, after `\CercleTrigo` of ProfLycee and `cercle-trigo` of
proflycee-minimal:

- `trig-circle` and `trig-circle-draw`: the circle, its remarkable values and
  angles, the frame (origin, ticks, vectors i and j, positive direction) and
  the real line;
- elements `tc-point` (with pgfplots marks), `tc-ray`, `tc-vector`, `tc-arc`,
  `tc-angle`, `tc-wind`, `tc-interval`, `tc-cos-eq`, `tc-sin-eq`,
  `tc-cos-sin`;
- names, points and arrow heads grow with the radius; marked points are drawn
  on top.
